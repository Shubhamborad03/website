The four agents

How each one actually works, in the simplest terms possible.

AGENT 01

DNSP Submission Agent

How it works
  • When a customer's deposit clears, the system grabs their address and works out which grid operator covers them (Energex, Ergon, Ausgrid, Endeavour, and so on)
  • It pulls the system details already in the job record (panels, inverter, system size, connection point)
  • Fills in that specific DNSP's pre-approval form
  • Submits the same day
  • Watches the portal and flags when the approval comes back, including any conditions imposed
What TSBA feels
  • Submissions today take someone 30 to 60 minutes per job. At 20 to 30 installs a month that's 10 to 30 hours of admin gone
  • Submissions today probably happen end-of-week in a batch. Now they happen same-day, which means approvals come back about a week earlier, install happens earlier, cash flow improves by roughly a week per deal
Real monthly value
  • 15 to 25 hours of admin time back · worth $750 to $1,250 at loaded cost
  • A week earlier on install for 25 deals · better cash flow + happier customers
AGENT 02

Warranty Manager

How it works
  • At install, the crew scans every panel, inverter, and battery serial number into the system on their phone
  • Warranty windows are tracked automatically per component
  • When a customer reports an issue, the agent gathers the system's diagnostic data from the monitoring portal and files the claim with the right manufacturer (Sungrow, Fronius, Tesla, Goodwe, etc)
  • Follows up at week 2 and week 4 if no reply
  • Sends the customer a status update at each step so they aren't left wondering
What TSBA feels
  • Warranty claims today probably take 3 to 4 weeks of dragged-out emails. With the agent chasing, that drops to 1 to 2 weeks
  • The customer is updated automatically, so the rep doesn't get the angry "what's happening" call on day 14
  • Reviews stay positive because the experience is smooth
Real monthly value
  • 10 to 30 hours of admin time back · worth $500 to $1,500 loaded
  • Fewer bad reviews · better reputation, more referrals (compounding effect)
AGENT 03

Inbox & Document Sorting

How it works
  • Every email landing in the main TSBA inbox is read by the agent
  • It works out what the email is about (supplier invoice, manufacturer reply, customer question, DNSP notice, council letter) and routes it to the right job folder
  • Attachments saved automatically
  • For routine emails (acknowledgements, status updates), it drafts a reply in the team's voice so they just tap send
What TSBA feels
  • The first 90 minutes of every morning currently spent triaging email becomes useful time
  • Nothing gets missed · the council notice that's currently buried for a week, the supplier invoice that goes overdue
  • The team always knows what's in their inbox at a glance
Real monthly value
  • 30 to 50 hours of admin time back across the team · worth $1,500 to $2,500
  • Things stop falling through cracks · harder to put a number on, but real
AGENT 04

Voice + SMS Front Door

How it works
  • An AI voice agent answers the TSBA business number 24/7
  • It speaks in your reps' style, asks the qualifying questions (single or three-phase, roof type, daytime power use, budget range, battery interest)
  • Books the consult straight into the right rep's calendar
  • For web form leads and Meta ad leads, an instant SMS goes out within 60 seconds with a personal booking link
What TSBA feels
  • The lead at 8pm Tuesday gets called back at 8:01pm, not 10am Wednesday
  • The lead from a Sunday ad gets a personal SMS in 60 seconds, not Monday morning
  • The team stops missing calls during installs or while on consult
  • Customer experience becomes "wow, these guys are fast" instead of "no one called me back"
Real monthly value · the big one
  • Industry research: 50%+ of solar leads contact 2 to 3 installers, and the first to respond wins the deal more often than not
  • A busy shop loses 8 to 15 leads per month to slow response
  • At $2,200 margin per deal that's $17,600 to $33,000 of recovered margin every month
  • Even if half holds up in reality, that's $8,800 to $16,500/month TSBA isn't currently getting
