How it works · Basic tier

Pick a suburb. Get a ranked list of roofs to quote.

Six steps, illustrated. Same flow on every tier. The difference is what tools and which AI does the work.

01

Roofer picks a suburb

From a dropdown in the dashboard. Anywhere in their service area.

02

AI looks at every house

Pulls satellite + Street View for each property and checks the roof.

03

AI filters out the ones already done

Checks council permits and recent sale photos. Drops anything that looks finished.

04

AI ranks the rest by likelihood

Top of the list = highest chance the roof actually needs attention.

05

Roofer markets to the list

Letterbox drop, Facebook ad to the postcode, cold call. Whoever responds becomes a lead.

06

AI quotes responders, roofer approves

Address in, branded quote out within minutes. One-tap approval. Customer gets the quote.
