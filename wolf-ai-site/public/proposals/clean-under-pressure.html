Fit

Who this is built for. And who it is not.

A fit assessment matters more than a pitch. Misaligned engagements waste operator time and Wolf AI's reputation.

This is for you

  • You provide residential pressure washing, soft washing, sealing, gutter cleaning, or related exterior services.
  • You convert enquiries via written quotes drafted from photographs or initial information.
  • Your average job value is $200 or more.
  • You have 12 or more months of trading history and 20 or more past quotes available.
  • Slow reply times are costing enquiries, or quote volume is exceeding admin capacity.

This is not for you

  • You operate commercial-only. A separate build and tuning is required.
  • You physically inspect every property before any quote is issued and never quote remotely.
  • You have less than 6 months of trading and no quote dataset.
  • You are seeking a chatbot to replace customer interaction. The Engine drafts quotes for operator approval. It does not replace operator communication.
  • Higher response volume is not a current priority.
Frequently asked

Questions raised before every engagement.

Pricing varies by surface type, access, and season. Can the system handle that?

The pricing logic captures conditional rules from your historical quotes. Surface type, area, access difficulty, severity, and seasonal modifiers are all incorporated. Accuracy is validated within ±10% before go-live.

What if the system drafts an incorrect quote?

No quote sends without operator approval. The approval card includes the price, the assumptions used, and a flag if any input falls outside normal range. The operator approves, edits, or escalates to a site visit.

A photo cannot show every condition. Access, hazards, additional surfaces.

Every quote includes a "subject to site inspection" clause by default. The booking link inside the quote leads to a confirmed site visit if the customer accepts. Final pricing is locked at inspection.

Does local outreach affect operator reputation?

Outreach is optional and disabled by default. When active, it sends drafted quotes to addresses with photo evidence and a real price band. The output is a quote, not a flyer. It can be disabled at any time.

Contract terms after the first month?

Month-to-month billing. One month's notice to cancel. No exit fee, no long-term contract.

Cost of pricing or service updates?

Template updates, pricing changes, and service additions are covered by the monthly fee. No additional invoicing for scope adjustments.

Can customers reach the operator directly through the quoting number?

Yes. Customer messages route to the operator's phone. The Engine does not block conversation. All threads are visible in the dashboard.

What happens at the monthly capacity ceiling?

Overflow rolls into the following month. Service does not stop mid-month. If the cap is reached consistently across consecutive months, a tier change is recommended at the next billing cycle.
