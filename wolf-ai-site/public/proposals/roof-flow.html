How it works

Pick a suburb. Get a ranked list of roofs to quote.

01

Roofer picks a suburb

From a dropdown in the dashboard. Anywhere in their service area.

02

AI looks at every house

Pulls satellite + Street View for each property and checks the roof.

03

AI filters out the ones already done

Checks council permits and recent sale photos. Drops anything that looks finished.

04

AI ranks the rest by likelihood

Top of the list = highest chance the roof actually needs attention.

05

Roofer markets to the list

Letterbox drop, Facebook ad to the postcode, cold call. Whoever responds becomes a lead.

06

AI quotes responders, roofer approves

Address in, branded quote out within minutes. One-tap approval. Customer gets the quote.

Nearmap sharing economics

If we share one Nearmap subscription across multiple clients.

Numbers above are per single client paying for their own Nearmap. In reality, one $250/mo Nearmap agency subscription serves multiple clients — splitting the cost. Other line items (LLM, SMS, Twilio, etc) stay per-client. This table isolates the Nearmap cost.

* Pro = $50 of non-Nearmap costs + Nearmap share. Killer = $83 of non-Nearmap costs + Nearmap share. Basic uses Google (free credit), so unaffected by Nearmap sharing — stays at $48/mo regardless.

Capacity note: $250/mo Nearmap agency tier estimated at ~30,000 image credits/month. 5 Pro clients (5×5,000 = 25,000 scans) fits. 3 Killer clients (3×10,000 = 30,000 scans) maxes it. Beyond that, upgrade to next Nearmap tier (~$500/mo for ~60k credits). Exact quota needs Nearmap sales call to confirm.
