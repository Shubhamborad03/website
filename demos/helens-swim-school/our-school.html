Helen's is not a chain or a council centre. It is a place and a person, run by the same family who started it in 1993, where we turn little splashes into big confidence.

The pool

A warm saltwater pool, open air under a roof.

Our pool sits on four acres of quiet bushland. It is treated with ozone and UV to keep it the best possible quality, soft on little eyes and skin, with none of the stinging chlorine or echo of a leisure centre.

It is heated consistently between 32 and 33 degrees right through the season, so a cold pool never stands between your child and a good lesson. Open air under a roof means fresh air and dappled light overhead, the bush all around, and a child who cannot wait for their next lesson.

Four acres of bushland

Some afternoons you will see the kangaroos grazing and the ducks on the dam.

Warm, soft saltwater, the bush all around, and a kookaburra on the fence sign to greet you at the gate. The bush is part of the lesson here, and the kids love it as much as the swimming. Nobody else on the Gold Coast can say that.

Miss Helen

One name on the gate, for over thirty years.

"We have been helping children grow into confident, capable swimmers since 1993."

Helen has taught a good part of the hinterland to swim. In 2018 she carried the Commonwealth Games baton, and called the honour of being a baton bearer an overwhelming, humbling experience, shared with some of the most incredible people.

That is the voice of the whole school: warm, modest and community rooted. Today she leads a small team of teachers, many of whom have been in this pool for years.
