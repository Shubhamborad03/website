The water

A warm, gentle pool kids actually want to get into.

No stinging chlorine and no echoing leisure centre. The pool is treated with ozone and UV to keep it the best possible quality, kept warm at 32 to 33 degrees all season, and open air under a roof so there is fresh air and dappled light overhead.

"It shouldn't matter how slowly a child learns, as long as we are encouraging them not to stop."

The class

Small classes, the same teacher, real progress.

This is what nervous parents come for. Morning lessons run with a maximum of three children per instructor, so your child is never lost in a crowd and never waiting their turn for long. They have the same teacher and the same fellow students each week, the way trust is built.

Our school

One woman's name on the gate, for over thirty years.

Helen's is not a chain or a council centre. It is a saltwater pool under an open air roof, run by the same family who started it in 1993. Helen carried the Commonwealth Games baton in 2018, and called it humbling.

The teachers go by Miss Helen, Miss Mel, Miss Sharon, Miss Esther, Miss Jade and Shane, and many of them have been in this pool for years. Your child has the same teacher every week, the way trust is built.

Four acres of bushland

The little swim school in the bush, and there is nowhere else like it.

Some afternoons you will see the kangaroos grazing and the ducks on the dam, and a kookaburra on the fence sign. Warm, soft saltwater, the bush all around, and a child who cannot wait for their next lesson. That is the whole idea.
