The water

A warm, gentle pool kids actually want to get into.

No stinging chlorine and no echoing leisure centre. Our pool is treated with ozone and UV to keep it the best possible quality, and kept warm all season so little ones stay calm and confident in the water.

  • Saltwater, ozone and UV

    Treated with ozone and UV, not harsh chlorine, so it is soft on little eyes and skin.

  • Warm, 32 to 33 degrees

    Heated consistently all season, so a cold pool never stands between your child and a good lesson.

  • Open air under roof

    Fresh air and dappled light, with a roof overhead, on four acres of quiet bushland.

  • Affiliated with Swim Australia

Our school

One woman's name on the gate, for thirty years.

"We have been helping children grow into confident, capable swimmers since 1993."

Helen's is not a chain or a council centre. It is a saltwater pool under an open air roof, on four acres of quiet bushland, run by the same family who started it. The teachers go by Miss Helen, Miss Mel, Miss Sharon, Miss Esther, Miss Jade and Shane, and many of them have been in this pool for years.

Helen carried the Commonwealth Games baton in 2018, and called it humbling.
