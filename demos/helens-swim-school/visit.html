Four acres of bushland

You will know you are in the right place when a kangaroo greets you at the gate.

Helen's is not a leisure centre. It is a saltwater pool under an open air roof, tucked into four acres of quiet Worongary bush, with kangaroos grazing on the path and ducks on the dam. Allow a few minutes to find us and soak it in.
