We love our job

The same friendly face, every rung of the way.

At Helen's your child has the same teacher and fellow students each week, guiding them up the ladder from a first splash to a confident swim. That is how trust is built. Meet the small team who teach the hinterland to swim.

Miss Helen, Principal

The name on the gate.

Helen has led the school since she founded it in 1993, and has taught a good part of the hinterland to swim. In 2018 she carried the Commonwealth Games baton, and called the honour humbling. Warm, modest and community rooted, she sets the tone for the whole team.

Why the same teacher matters

Trust is built one familiar week at a time.

With a maximum of three children per teacher in the mornings, your child is never lost in a crowd and never waiting long for their turn. And with the same teacher every week, they get someone who knows them by name and knows exactly where they are up to on the ladder.

Each week we award Swimmers of the Week for improvement, for displaying courage, or for showing respect and kindness to others. Sometimes the biggest win is just putting your face in.

"It shouldn't matter how slowly a child learns, as long as we are encouraging them not to stop."
