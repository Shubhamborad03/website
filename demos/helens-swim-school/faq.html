Ages & classes

Who we teach, and how small the classes are.

The short version: from 4 months old, a maximum of 3 children per teacher in the mornings, and 30 minute lessons.

From what age can my child start?

From 4 months and up. Gentle parent and baby classes begin at four months, and lessons continue right through to confident young swimmers.

How many children are in a class?

Morning lessons run with a maximum of 3 children per instructor, so your child is never lost in a crowd. After school classes are kept small too: 3 in beginner classes, 4 for stroke development, and up to 6 in Squad.

How long is a lesson?

Lessons are 30 minutes. Squad sessions run for 45 minutes.

Will my child have the same teacher each week?

Yes. Your child has the same teacher and the same fellow students each week. That is how trust is built, the same friendly face guiding them on their aquatic adventure.

The pool

Warm, soft saltwater, open air under a roof.

Heated to 32 to 33 degrees, treated with ozone and UV rather than harsh chlorine, on four acres of quiet bushland.

How warm is the pool?

The pool is heated consistently between 32 and 33 degrees, all season, so a cold pool never stands between your child and a good lesson.

Is it chlorine?

No. Our pool is saltwater, treated with ozone and UV to keep it the best possible quality. It is soft on little eyes and skin, with no stinging chlorine.

Is the pool indoors or outdoors?

It is open air under a roof. You get fresh air and dappled light with shade overhead, on four acres of bushland. Some afternoons you will see the kangaroos grazing and the ducks on the dam.

Are you accredited?

We are affiliated with Swim Australia, and we have been teaching the hinterland to swim since 1993.

What to bring

Pack light, arrive ready to swim.

A short list keeps the morning calm. Little ones not yet toilet trained will need a swim nappy.

  • Swimwear

    On under their clothes is easiest, so there is less to manage poolside.

  • A towel

    Plus something warm for after the lesson on cooler mornings.

  • A swim nappy

    For any child not yet toilet trained. This one is essential.

  • Goggles, if they have them

    Not required to start. Plenty of children begin without them.

Make up lessons

Miss a lesson? You can make it up.

Give us at least 2 hours notice and the lesson is held as a credit to make up later.

How do I let you know we cannot make it?

Let us know through the family portal at least 2 hours before the lesson. With that notice the lesson is credited as a make up.

Do make up lessons expire?

Yes. Make up credits are valid for 120 days from the missed lesson, so they are best used while the season is running.

Is there a charge for a make up lesson?

No. Make up lessons are free when you give the required notice.

Bookings & payments

A permanent weekly spot, billed monthly.

$26.50 per child per lesson, your first lesson free, and a sibling discount for larger families.

How does booking work?

We take permanent bookings only, a regular weekly spot rather than casual one off lessons. That weekly time is held for your child each week of the season.

How much is a lesson?

$26.50 per child per lesson, and your first lesson is free. A family discount applies for three or more children: the third child is $18 and the fourth is $8. Monthly invoices are capped at $132 per child, and there is no charge on public holidays.

How and when do I pay?

We invoice monthly by direct debit. Current families manage bookings, make up lessons and account details in the family portal.

Health & seasons

When we run, and when to stay home.

Lessons run January to May and August to mid December, with a rest over winter and Christmas.

When does the school run?

Our seasons run January to May and August to mid December. We close over winter and the Christmas break, because everyone deserves a rest, and we will see you all refreshed for the next season.

What if my child is unwell?

Please keep your child home if they are unwell, and let us know so we can arrange a make up lesson. To protect the other little ones in the pool, children should not swim while sick. If they have had a tummy upset, keep them out of the water until they have been clear for the period set out in our health policy.

What happens on public holidays?

There is no charge for lessons that fall on a public holiday.
