The details

  • Where

    180 Gympie Terrace, Noosaville QLD 4566. On the river foreshore, next to the Big Pelican, Noosa Ferry Stop 3.

  • When

    Open 7 days, year round. Coffee at the Riverside Kiosk from 7:30am. Boat hire 8am to 5pm.

  • Phone

    07 5449 7239

  • Email

    info@pelicanboathire.com.au

  • Fuel

    The only Code compliant fuel station, and the only diesel outlet, on the Noosa River.
