How it works

Honest and simple

  • Any value

    Choose the dollar amount that suits. The recipient can put it toward any hire, and top up the difference if they want a bigger day.

  • How to order

    Call us on 07 5449 7239 or email info@pelicanboathire.com.au. Tell us the value and whether you would like it posted, emailed or picked up at the shop.

    The recipient books their day with us by phone or in person, then uses the card against the hire. Same friendly brief, same no licence needed up to 12 aboard.
