Noosa's Favourite! · Calm water, no surf, no crowds

Once you cast off, the river's yours.

Fish the quiet spots, fire up the BBQ on board, beach at a sandbank for a swim, or just drift the ti-tree water all day. Rods and a river map come with the boat.

The complete river day

We'll show you the good spots.

“Let us show you the best fishing spots and the nicest places to picnic and swim.” Pick a boat, and we hand you a waterproof map of where to go and where to keep clear of the jetskis.

On the water

Four ways to spend the day.

Every Pelican boat is set up for a proper day out, not a quick lap. Here's what people actually do once they're on the river.

Go fishing

Rods come with the boat, and the team marks the best spots on your river map. Flathead, bream and whiting all run in the Noosa River. Grab bait and tackle from the shop on your way out.

“We had an awesome day on the hire boat. 9 of us went. Rods and BBQ supplied.”Amanda P, TripAdvisor

Pick a fishing boat

BBQ on board

Every BBQ pontoon carries a clean gas barbie. Bring bacon and eggs for a mid-river brunch, or snags and steaks for lunch, and cook it all while you drift. No licence needed up to 12 aboard.

“Took bacon and eggs for brunch, cooked on very clean bbq.”lbcp13, TripAdvisor

See the BBQ pontoons

Picnic & swim at a sandbank

Beach the boat on a quiet sandbank, lay out the picnic and swim in the calm, shallow water. We'll point you to the nicest spots to pull up, away from the crowds and the surf.

“A glorious Easter Day spent on the Noosa River. Our boat was clean, comfortable and ran well.”Jacky M, Brisbane, TripAdvisor

Pick your boat

Explore by paddle

Slow it right down on a kayak or stand-up paddleboard. The river's calm and ti-tree-stained, perfect for first-timers, and we run lessons off the jetty if you've never paddled before.

“We spent a great hour out on the Noosa river. You can't miss the giant pelican out front.”RockieHazel, TripAdvisor

See kayaks & SUP

Bring the whole mob

The kids, the grandparents, and the dog.

Big groups, little ones, family home from overseas, the dog, it all fits on a Pelican boat. Pontoons take up to 21 for the big occasions, and there's a friendly safety brief before you go so first-timers feel sorted.

“With family home from overseas, me on my first trip after major surgery and three large dogs, we booked a pontoon BBQ for four hours and had a wonderful time on the Noosa River.”EileenW1, TripAdvisor

What's included

You bring the food. We've got the rest.

Every hire comes with fishing rods, a waterproof river map, life jackets and a clear safety brief so you know where to go and what to avoid. BBQ pontoons add a clean gas barbie. Top up unleaded or diesel at our own fuel dock, the only one on the river, before you head out.

Just a car driver's licence is needed for any boat carrying up to 12 people, that's nearly the whole fleet. Pontoon hire is for drivers 25 and over.
