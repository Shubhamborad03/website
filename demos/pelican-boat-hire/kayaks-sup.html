Noosa's Favourite! · Quiet water at your own pace

Kayaks & stand-up paddle on calm water.

The Noosa River is ti-tree-stained and still, no surf, no chop. Slide a kayak or a board off our jetty and the morning's yours. No licence needed, and no booking fuss, just turn up.

★ 4.7 from 323 Google reviews From $20 the first hour Open 7 days · hire 8am to 5pm

Flat, tannin-brown water that barely moves, the easiest paddle in Noosa, straight off our own jetty.

This stretch of the Noosa River is calm all day. That makes it forgiving for first-timers and kids, and lovely for a quiet early paddle before the boats are out. Single kayaks, double kayaks for two, and stand-up paddleboards are all ready on the foreshore at 180 Gympie Terrace.

No need to plan it days ahead. Plenty of people just walk up, the way one group of nine did, and head out for an hour on the water.

Easy as it sounds

No licence. No surf. No fuss.

You don't need any licence to paddle, and you don't need to book ahead. The Noosa River here is calm, sheltered water, no waves to fight, so it suits kids, beginners and anyone who just wants a quiet hour. Life jackets supplied, and the team will point you to the best spots.
