Noosa's Favourite! · The river, in real photos

A day on the Noosa River, the way it really looks.

No stock, no staging. The fleet at our jetty, pontoons beached on the sandbank, paddlers on the calm ti-tree water, and the Big Pelican out front. All shot right here on Gympie Terrace.

The sandbank

Where the river meets the sea

Beach the pontoon on the sandbank at the river mouth, fire up the barbie, and the kids run free. This is the day most people come back for.

The fleet

Boats and BBQ pontoons

From a simple runabout to the big yellow-and-black BBQ pontoons, every boat is hired off the same jetty we have used since 1957.

Quiet water

Kayaks, paddleboards and the calm river

No surf, no crowds. Just tannin-stained water, moored yachts in the distance and the whole river at your own pace.

On Gympie Terrace

The shop, the kiosk and the Big Pelican

You cannot miss us. The yellow shopfront, the coffee kiosk from 7:30am, the rods and life jackets ready to go, and the giant pelican the whole river navigates by.

Golden hour

When the river goes quiet

The Noosa ferry crossing our stretch as the sun drops behind the hills. Nearly seventy years on, this is still our favourite time on the water.

Your turn on the water
