The licence rules

Do you need a boat licence?

Short version: almost never. A car driver's licence covers nearly the whole fleet. Here is exactly how it breaks down.

No licence, up to 12

Any boat carrying up to 12 people needs only a current car driver's licence. That is nearly every boat we hire, from the runabout to the BBQ pontoons.

Pontoons, 25 and over

To drive a BBQ pontoon, the person at the wheel must be 25 years or older and hold a driver's licence. Still no boat licence required.

Over 12, rec licence

Our 21-seat River Explorer carries more than 12 passengers, so the driver needs a recreational boat licence. The only boat in the fleet that does.

Good to know

The honest fine print

  • Booking

    There is no live calendar here. Check availability and book direct with us by phone on 07 5449 7239 or in person at the shop. Book direct and your money stays on the river, not with an online middleman.

  • Hire times

    Boat hire runs 8am to 5pm, seven days a year round. The Riverside Kiosk opens for coffee from 7:30am.

  • Minimum hire

    On weekends and peak periods a four hour minimum may apply to the larger pontoons. We will let you know when you book.

  • Who can drive

    The driver must hold a current driver's licence. Pontoon drivers must be 25 or over. The 21-seat River Explorer needs a recreational boat licence.

  • Weather

    The Noosa River is calm, sheltered water, not the surf coast, so most days are good days. If conditions are unsafe we will talk you through the options when you book.

  • What to bring

    Your driver's licence, food and drinks, sunscreen and a hat. We supply the rods, the map, the safety gear and a clean BBQ. Dogs and big groups are welcome.
