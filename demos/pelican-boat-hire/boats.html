Pick your boat by how many you're bringing. That's how everyone chooses, so that's how it's laid out.

Every boat is hired off our own jetty at 180 Gympie Terrace, the same jetty Pelican has run since 1957. Fishing rods come aboard, you get a waterproof map of the good spots and where to keep clear of the jetskis, and the bigger boats carry a clean gas barbie so you can cook brunch mid-river.

Before you cast off, the team walks you through everything, where to go, what to watch, how to drive it. First-timers are welcome, and that's most of who we hire to.

The question everyone asks

Do I need a boat licence?

No.

A car driver's licence is all you need for any boat carrying up to 12 people, that's nearly the whole fleet. Pontoon hire is for drivers 25 and over. The only boats that need a recreational boat licence are the 16-seat pontoon and the 21-seat River Explorer, which carry more than 12.
