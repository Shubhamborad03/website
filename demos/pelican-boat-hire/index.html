The question everyone asks

Do I need a boat licence?

No.

A car driver's licence is all you need for any boat carrying up to 12 people, that's nearly the whole fleet. Pontoon hire is for drivers 25 and over. The only exception is our 21-seat River Explorer, which carries more than 12, so it needs a recreational boat licence.

Noosa's favourite, since 1957

Noosa's favourite boat hire company

“We're a family owned business and we live on the river. Our kids have grown up on the river, and we enjoy everything it has to offer.”

from our About page

For nearly seventy years, one family has been the Noosa River's infrastructure, not a counter on the foreshore, the thing the river runs on.

  • The slipway

    Where it started in 1957, a working slipway and boat repairs, still here on the same jetty.

  • The fuel dock

    The only Code-compliant fuel station, and the only diesel outlet, on the entire Noosa River.

  • The Riverside Kiosk

    Coffee from 7:30am and an information centre on the foreshore, open to everyone, boat or no boat.

  • The Big Pelican

    The giant pelican out front is the landmark the whole river navigates by. “You can't miss the giant pelican out front.”

  1. 1957

    Established as Noosa Marine Services, slipway, small boat repairs, and ‘tinnies' hired out as Pelican Hire Boats.

  2. 2008

    The old building stripped to a bare shell and rebuilt for the next fifty years.

  3. 2016

    A modern retail outlet with kiosk and information centre, a restored jetty, and new fuel systems.

  4. 2021 to 2022

    Queensland Tourism Awards Gold, two years running, Best Retail & Hire business in Queensland. Bronze again in 2023, and Bronze at the Australian Tourism Awards. As featured on Getaway.
