Noosa's Favourite! · A skipper at the wheel

Charters. Let someone else drive the boat.

Some days you just want to sit back. Our charter boats, the Day Dreamer and the Day Tripper, come with someone at the wheel so the whole crew can relax. As one of our owners puts it, “if you want to really relax, ask us about providing a captain for your boat.”

★ 4.7 from 323 Google reviews Planned with you by phone Open 7 days · on the Noosa River

A charter is the relaxed way to do the river, no driving, no watching the channel markers, just the day.

For a special occasion, a celebration, or simply a day where nobody in the group has to skipper, a charter is the answer. You're aboard one of our charter boats with someone who knows the Noosa River, the quiet sandbanks, the swimming spots, where the pelicans gather.

Because every charter is shaped around your day, the size of your group, the hours, the occasion, we plan it with you over the phone rather than quoting a one-size price online. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll build the day around it.

The charter boats

Day Dreamer & Day Tripper

Two boats built for an easy day on the water. Pick the feel you're after, then call and we'll plan the rest with you.

The relaxed cruise

Day Dreamer

Slow it right down. The Day Dreamer is for a gentle drift along the river, pulling up at a quiet sandbank for a swim and letting the afternoon go by.

  • Skippered, so you don't drive
  • Calm Noosa River, no surf
  • Sandbank swim stops
  • Shaded seating aboard

The explore-the-river day

Day Tripper

Cover more water. The Day Tripper is for the group that wants to get out and see the river, beach the boat, explore the foreshore, and make a real day trip of it.

  • Skippered, so you don't drive
  • More of the river covered
  • Beach stops along the way
  • Great for a group occasion

How charter pricing works

Planned with you, by phone.

We don't list a fixed charter price, because no two charters are the same. The cost depends on your group, your hours and your occasion, so we put it together with you on a quick call. No obligation, no online middleman, just a straight conversation about the day you want.
