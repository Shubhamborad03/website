Noosa's Favourite! · On the river since 1957

One family. One jetty. Since 1957.

We don't just hire boats. We're the river. For nearly seventy years one family has lived on this stretch of the Noosa River, and built the thing the whole river runs on.

In their own words

We live on the river.

“We're a family owned business and we live on the river. Our kids have grown up on the river, and we enjoy everything it has to offer.”

Where it started

From a slipway and a row of tinnies.

It began in 1957 as Noosa Marine Services, a working slipway and small boat repairs on the foreshore, with a handful of tinnies hired out under the Pelican name. The river was quieter then. The family wasn't.

The same jetty, all these years.

Everything Pelican does still happens off the same jetty at 180 Gympie Terrace. The slipway that started it. The boats that go out every morning. The kiosk where the coffee's on by 7:30. Three generations have grown up on this water, and it shows in how the place is run.

It's a family owned business, and the family lives on the river. The kids learned to drive a boat here before they could drive a car. That's not a marketing line, it's just the truth of the place.

Not a counter on the foreshore. The thing the river runs on.

Pelican grew into the Noosa River's infrastructure. The only Code-compliant fuel station, and the only diesel outlet, on the entire river. A working slipway for repairs and hauling out. A riverside kiosk and information centre open to everyone, boat or no boat. And the giant pelican out front that the whole river navigates by.

It's the difference between renting a boat and being handed the keys to the river by the people who know it best.

Nearly seventy years

The long version.

Rebuilt twice, never moved. Here's how one family kept the river's front door open through seven decades.

  1. 1957

    Noosa Marine Services opens.

    A slipway and small boat repairs on the Noosa River foreshore, with a row of tinnies hired out as Pelican Hire Boats. The start of everything that's still here.

  2. 2008

    Stripped to a bare shell, rebuilt.

    The old building was taken back to its bones and rebuilt for the next fifty years, the same family, the same river, a stronger home for it.

  3. 2016

    A modern retail outlet, kiosk and restored jetty.

    A full rebuild brought a modern shop, the Riverside Kiosk and information centre, a restored jetty and new fuel systems, the Pelican most visitors know today.

  4. 2021

    Queensland Tourism Awards, Gold.

    Named Best Retail and Hire business in Queensland, and Bronze at the Australian Tourism Awards the same year.

  5. 2022

    Gold again, two years running.

    A second Queensland Tourism Awards Gold, back to back. Bronze followed in 2023. The family business that became the most awarded hire operator on the river.
