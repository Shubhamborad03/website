The Gosford Inn Motel sits in a quiet, tranquil bushland setting, nestled between the magnificent Brisbane Water and picturesque national parks, set back from the road so the nights are quiet. It is a two-storey, family-run motel, the kind of place where a pleasant voice answers the phone and the booking gets done with no fuss.

We are honest about what we are. Obviously basic, but it provides all that you need, neat, clean and tidy, with comfortable beds and crisp white sheets. Guests do not come here for luxury, and we never pretend otherwise. They come for a good night’s sleep, fair value, and people who do everything with a smile. That is the promise, and more than a thousand reviews say we keep it.

Most of our guests are people passing through, hospital visits, an event at the stadium, tradies and workers on the job, families breaking up the long drive between Sydney and Newcastle, and dog owners glad to find somewhere that will take them. We are minutes from Gosford station, the hospital and Central Coast Stadium, with easy access to the M1, and the beaches at Terrigal and The Entrance a short drive on.

The thing that sets us apart is downstairs. The same family that runs the motel runs Don’t Tell Mumma, the on-site Indian restaurant that guests rate among the best Indian food on the coast. There is a licensed bar, you can dine in, or, as guests love to mention, have dinner brought right to your room. Read more about the restaurant →
