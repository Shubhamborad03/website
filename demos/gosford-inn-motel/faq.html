Gosford Inn/FAQ

Good to know

The things guests ask, answered plainly.

Check-in times, parking, WiFi, the pool, pets and how to book direct. If your question isn’t here, the desk is happy to help on 02 4328 1222.

Arriving and leavingCheck-in, check-out, reception

What time is check-in and check-out? +

Check-in is from 2:00 pm. Check-out is by 10:00 am.

Reception is open 8:00 am to 7:30 pm, every day.

Can I arrive late or after reception closes? +

Yes. Just let us know first. Call the desk on 02 4328 1222 during reception hours, or the after-hours number 0491 677 961, and we’ll sort out your key. Guests tell us late check-in is “a breeze”.

Is the motel quiet? +

The motel sits back from the Pacific Highway in bushland, which guests mention often: “set back from the road so very quiet.” It is an honest older building, so if you’re a light sleeper, ask for a ground-floor room when you book.

Parking, WiFi and the poolOn site

Is parking free? +

Yes. Parking is free and right at your door.

Is there WiFi? +

Yes, WiFi is free for guests.

Is the pool open? +

The outdoor pool is seasonal, open roughly October to May. It’s closed over the colder months, so call to confirm it’s open for your dates if a swim is part of the plan.

Is there anywhere to cook or have a drink? +

There’s a guest BBQ gazebo on the grounds, and the on-site Indian restaurant Don’t Tell Mumma with a licensed bar. You can dine in, sit at the bar, or have dinner brought to your room.

Pets, booking and locationThe rest

Are pets allowed? +

Ask about pet-friendly rooms when you book on 02 4328 1222. We’ll let you know what’s available and any conditions.

How do I book direct? +

Call the desk on 02 4328 1222 for tonight’s best rate, or book online through the motel’s own secure booking engine on the Contact page.

Booking direct keeps the full rate with the family business and skips the fees the booking sites add, and you talk to the people who’ll actually hand you the key.

Where is the motel, and what’s nearby? +

733 Pacific Highway, Narara NSW 2250, on the Central Coast. It’s about a five-minute drive to Gosford station, Gosford Hospital and Central Coast Stadium, with easy access to the M1 between Sydney and Newcastle.

It’s a quiet bushland setting rather than a walk-to-town spot, so you’ll want the car for shops, clubs and the beaches (Terrigal is about 10 km, The Entrance about 14 km).

What are the rooms like? +

Clean, comfortable and honest 3-star. Crisp white sheets, comfortable beds, a kettle, a microwave, TV, heating and air-con. As one guest put it, “obviously basic but provides all that you need and neat, clean and tidy.” Bathrooms are compact and on the older side. If you want luxury, we’re not it, but for clean, comfortable and great value, guests come back. See the rooms and gallery.
