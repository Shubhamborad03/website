Everything you need, nothing you don’t

A pool, a feed, parking at your door.

The Gosford Inn Motel keeps it practical. A seasonal pool out the back, free parking right at your room, free WiFi, a restaurant and bar downstairs, and a guest BBQ under the gazebo. Obviously basic, and proud of it, the things that make a stop comfortable are all here.

The pool

A seasonal pool out the back.

An outdoor pool surrounded by palms, a fantastic bonus on a warm Central Coast afternoon, as more than one guest has put it. Open through the warmer months, October to May.

Please note: the pool is closed over winter (late May to the end of September).

Restaurant & bar

Don’t Tell Mumma, downstairs.

The same family runs the motel and the Indian restaurant on site, “the best Indian food on the coast” by guest ratings. There’s a licensed bar, you can dine in, or have dinner brought to your door. “oh and they even serve the BEST tasting Indian meal to your room,” one guest wrote on her tenth stay.

More about Don’t Tell Mumma →

Guest BBQ

A covered BBQ gazebo.

A shaded gazebo with the BBQ and outdoor seating, for when you’d rather cook your own and eat in the fresh air. Handy for longer stays and bigger groups in the apartment.

Parking, WiFi, charging

Pull in, plug in, settle.

Free parking right at your door, the easy kind guests mention. Free WiFi throughout. Room service if you don’t feel like moving. And a Tesla EV charger on site for $20 a charge, a quiet stop with a full battery in the morning.

EV charging is $20 per charge, ask at reception.
