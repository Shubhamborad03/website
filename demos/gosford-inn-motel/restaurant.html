The Indian restaurant downstairs

Don’t Tell Mumma

The best curry on the Coast is downstairs from your room. The same family runs the motel and the kitchen, so the welcome at the desk and the food at the table come from the same people. Licensed bar. Dine in, or have dinner brought to your door.

Three ways to eat tonight

i

To your room

The guest favourite. Order from reception or by phone and dinner comes to your door, no need to find the car keys after a long drive.

ii

Dine in

Sit down to a proper meal a few steps from your room. A warm, no-fuss room run by the family who run the motel.

iii

At the licensed bar

A cold drink with the meal, or just a quiet one after check-in. Fully licensed, so the wine and the beer are sorted.

On the menu

Classic North Indian cooking, the curries, the breads, the rice and the sides that guests come back for. The kitchen’s hours move with the season, so the surest way to know what’s on tonight is to ask the desk.
